How it works

怎么用

  1. Describe the decision you are facing.
  2. Pick the models and principles that apply.
  3. Write your reasoning, the trade-offs, and what would change your mind.
  4. Come back later and record what actually happened.
  1. 写下你正在面对的决定。
  2. 挑出适用的思维模型与准则。
  3. 记录你的推理、权衡，以及什么会改变你的想法。
  4. 过一段时间回来，补记实际发生了什么。

Private by design

隐私优先

  • No account. Open the app and start writing.
  • Your writing never leaves the device. Only purchase and entitlement checks go to Apple and RevenueCat.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, ads, tracking, or third-party SDKs beyond RevenueCat for purchases.
  • Encrypted on device. Your journal is encrypted with a key held in the iOS Keychain.
  • Yours to leave with. Export or delete your data whenever you want; uninstalling removes the local vault.
  • 无账号。打开就能写。
  • 你写的内容不离开设备。只有购买与权益校验会与 Apple、RevenueCat 通信。
  • 无统计、无崩溃上报、无广告、不追踪；除内购校验用的 RevenueCat 外，无其他第三方 SDK。
  • 本机加密。日记加密保存，密钥存放在 iOS Keychain。
  • 随时可带走。你可以自行导出或删除数据；卸载 App 即清除本地加密库。

Pro, if you want it

关于 Pro

The journal and the built-in library are usable without paying. Pro is an optional way to support an independent, local-first app and future library updates. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically; a one-time lifetime purchase is also available. Payment is processed by Apple, and your entitlement is verified with RevenueCat; prices are shown in the App Store.

日记与内置内容库无需付费即可使用。Pro 是可选的支持方式，用于支持这个独立、本机优先的产品与后续内容更新。月付与年付会自动续期，也提供一次性买断。付款由 Apple 处理，权益由 RevenueCat 校验；价格以 App Store 显示为准。
