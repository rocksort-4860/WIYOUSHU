Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题

Q: Where my decision data stored? Can data get lost?

Q：我的决策数据保存在哪里？会不会丢失？

All your decision‑journal data stays locally on device and never upload to developer server. Uninstalling app will erase local‑data completely. It’s strongly recommended to export CSV regularly or enable iCloud‑Drive backup feature.

全部决策数据保存在本机，不会上传开发者服务器。卸载应用会彻底删除本地全部数据；强烈建议定期导出CSV，或者开启iCloud云盘备份。

Q: How does iCloud‑Drive backup work?

Q：iCloud云盘备份如何工作？

After you turn‑on inside App settings, App writes timestamped backup‑files into your own iCloud‑Drive. Each backup generates new separate file. You can view or delete these backup files inside iOS "Files" App, we cannot read them.

在设置内开启后，本应用向你的iCloud云盘写入带时间戳备份文件；每次备份生成独立新文件；你可以在iOS「文件」App查看、删除备份，开发者无法读取。

Q: How can I erase all my local data?

Q：如何清除本机全部数据？

Open Settings → Data Security → "Delete all data" and confirm. This will wipe all decision entries, cancel pending review reminders, delete backup files created by App (both local sandbox and iCloud‑Drive), reset App to empty state. Your language, theme and purchased entitlement remain. This action cannot undo. You can also delete single entry inside App or uninstall application.

打开设置‑数据安全‑删除全部数据确认执行；清除全部决策条目，取消待执行回访提醒，删除应用生成本地与iCloud备份，重置应用；语言、主题、已购权益保留；操作不可撤销；也可以单条删除记录或直接卸载App。

Q: About 7‑day free‑trial & subscription cancellation

Q：关于7天免费试用与订阅取消

7‑day free‑trial only applies to monthly / yearly subscriptions, lifetime purchase does not include trial. If you do not wish to be charged, please cancel subscription at least 24 hours before trial‑expire date. Manage or cancel subscription in iOS Settings → Your Apple‑ID → Subscriptions.

7天免费试用仅针对月付、年付订阅；终身买断不提供试用。如果你不想产生扣费，请务必在试用到期前至少24小时取消订阅。前往iOS设置 → 你的Apple ID → 订阅页面管理或取消。
