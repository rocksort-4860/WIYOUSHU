Frequently asked questions

常见问题

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device, in encrypted local storage. The encryption key is held in the iOS Keychain. The app has no server and no account. Apart from purchase and entitlement checks, it makes no network requests. If you use iCloud or iTunes device backups, your backup is controlled by Apple and by your own backup settings.
How do I export or back up my journal?
Open Settings → Backup & Restore → Export backup. You get plain text that you can copy, save, or share. Once exported, the text is no longer encrypted, so store it somewhere you trust. Anyone who has that text can read it.
Can I restore a purchase on a new iPhone?
Yes. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID, open the Pro screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not to the device. The app then checks your entitlement with RevenueCat using an anonymous App User ID and your purchase record; it never sees your payment details.
How do I cancel a subscription?
iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. There is no free trial; the plan starts charging when you confirm the purchase. You can also manage or cancel from the Pro screen’s “manage subscription” instructions.
How do I request a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use Apple’s report a problem page at reportaproblem.apple.com, or contact Apple Support. We cannot issue or reverse a charge ourselves.
How do I delete everything?
Open Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete all data, then confirm. This removes your local entries and any app-generated export files. It cannot be undone. Your purchase record stays with Apple. Uninstalling the app also removes the local vault and its key.
Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Writing, the built-in library, search, and export all work offline. Only a purchase, a restore, or an entitlement check needs a connection: Apple processes the payment and RevenueCat verifies your Pro entitlement.
Are the models and cases advice?
No. They are thinking frameworks and references. They do not account for your circumstances and are not medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice. For decisions in those areas, consult a qualified professional.
Why does part of the built-in library appear in Chinese?
Some historical samples were written in Chinese first and their English versions are still being added. English mode falls back to the original Chinese for those entries rather than showing a machine translation. Everything you write yourself is always shown exactly as you typed it.
我的数据存在哪里？
只存在你的设备上，采用本地加密存储，加密密钥存放在 iOS Keychain。App 没有服务器、没有账号；除购买与权益校验外，不发网络请求。如果你使用 iCloud 或 iTunes 的设备备份，该备份由 Apple 和你的备份设置决定。
如何导出或备份？
设置 → 备份与恢复 → 导出备份。你会得到一份可复制、保存或分享的纯文本。导出后的文本不再加密，请保存在你信任的地方；拿到这段文本的人都能读取内容。
换新 iPhone 后如何恢复购买？
可以。用同一 Apple ID 登录 App Store，打开 Pro 页面，点击“恢复购买”。购买绑定在你的 Apple ID 上，不绑定设备。App 随后会用 RevenueCat 校验权益，只使用匿名 App 用户 ID 与购买记录，不会看到你的支付信息。
如何取消订阅？
iOS 设置 → 你的 Apple ID → 订阅。仅删除 App 不会取消订阅。请在续期日前至少 24 小时取消，以免产生下一次扣费。本产品没有免费试用，确认购买后即开始计费。你也可以在 Pro 页面的订阅说明里查看管理与取消方式。
如何申请退款？
退款由 Apple 处理，不由我们处理。请使用 Apple 的“报告问题”页面 reportaproblem.apple.com，或联系 Apple 支持。我们无法自行发起或撤销扣款。
如何删除全部数据？
设置 → 数据与隐私 → 清空全部数据，然后确认。这会删除本地条目与 App 生成的导出文件，且不可撤销。购买记录仍保留在 Apple。卸载 App 也会清除本地加密库及其密钥。
App 需要联网吗？
不需要。写作、内置内容库、检索与导出都可离线使用。只有购买、恢复购买或权益校验需要联网：付款由 Apple 处理，Pro 权益由 RevenueCat 校验。
模型和案例是建议吗？
不是。它们只是思考框架与参考，不考虑你的具体处境，也不构成医疗、心理、法律或财务建议。涉及这些领域时，请咨询具备资质的专业人士。
为什么内置内容库有一部分是中文？
部分历史样本最初以中文写成，英文版本仍在补充。英文模式下这些条目会回退显示中文原文，而不是机器翻译。你自己写下的内容始终按原文显示。
