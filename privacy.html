Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Decision Journal is built so that your entries never leave your device.

Decision Journal 的设计前提是：你写下的内容永远不离开你的设备。

Effective: 2026-11-06 · Last updated: 2026-11-08

生效日期：2026-11-06 · 最后更新：2026-11-08

The short version: your journal stays encrypted on your device. There is no account, no server for your entries, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking. If you buy Pro, Apple processes the payment and RevenueCat receives an anonymous App User ID and your purchase record solely to verify your entitlement.

一句话说明：你写的内容以加密形式留在你的设备上。没有账号、没有存放内容的服务器、没有统计、没有崩溃上报、没有广告，也不追踪你。如果你购买 Pro，付款由 Apple 处理，RevenueCat 会接收一个匿名 App 用户 ID 与购买记录，仅用于校验你的会员权益。

1. What we collect

1. 我们收集什么

We do not collect your journal content. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share anything you write. The app does not create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, or health data. The only purchase-related data is described in section 4.

我们不收集你写的内容。我们不收集、不传输、不售卖、不共享你写下的任何内容。App 不创建账号，也不会索取你的姓名、邮箱、通讯录、位置、照片、麦克风、相机或健康数据。唯一涉及购买的数据见第 4 节。

2. Where your entries are stored

2. 你的内容存在哪里

Journals, decision records, notes, and settings are encrypted on your device before they are written to local app storage. The master key is held in the iOS Keychain; the Android internal test build uses Keystore-backed secure storage. The ciphertext stays inside the app's own container.

日记、决策记录、笔记与设置会先在设备上加密，再写入本地 App 存储。主密钥保存在 iOS Keychain；安卓内测版本使用 Keystore 支持的加密存储。密文只保存在 App 自己的容器内。

We do not operate a server and cannot access, read, decrypt, or recover your entries. The app has no sync feature. If you enable an iOS device backup (including iCloud Backup), that backup is controlled by Apple and your iOS settings, not by us.

我们不运营服务器，无法访问、读取、解密或恢复你的内容。App 没有同步功能。如果你启用了 iOS 设备备份（包括 iCloud 备份），该备份由 Apple 与你的 iOS 设置控制，不由我们控制。

If you use the in-app export feature, the app produces a plain-text backup and hands it to the iOS share sheet or clipboard at your explicit request. From that point the file is outside the app's protected storage; where it goes is your choice and your responsibility. You can delete those exported files wherever you saved them — the app keeps no copy.

如果你使用 App 内的导出功能，App 会生成一份明文备份，并在你明确操作后交给 iOS 分享面板或剪贴板。从那一刻起，文件就离开了 App 的受保护存储；它去向何处由你选择，也由你负责。你可以在保存的位置自行删除这些导出文件；App 不保留副本。

3. Network, analytics, crash reporting and ads

3. 网络、统计、崩溃上报与广告

The only network activity comes from purchases and entitlement checks: Apple processes payments and RevenueCat verifies whether your Pro entitlement is active (section 4). The app makes no other network requests. It contains no analytics service, no crash-reporting service, no advertising network, and no attribution SDK. RevenueCat does not receive anything you write in the app. No data is used to track you across apps or websites.

唯一的网络活动来自购买与权益校验：Apple 处理付款，RevenueCat 校验你的 Pro 权益是否生效（见第 4 节）。除此之外，App 不发任何网络请求。它不包含统计服务、崩溃上报服务、广告网络或归因 SDK。RevenueCat 不会收到你在 App 里写下的任何内容。我们不使用任何数据跨 App 或跨网站追踪你。

4. Purchases

4. 内购

Monthly, yearly, and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple receives the purchase information needed to complete the transaction under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details. To verify whether your entitlement is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous App User ID, the purchased product identifiers, and entitlement status under RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. This data is used only to validate your Pro entitlement, is not linked to your journal content, and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. We do not operate a server, and the app does not send your receipt or journal to us.

月付、年付与买断由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。Apple 会依据 Apple 隐私政策接收完成交易所需的信息。我们永远看不到你的支付信息。为校验会员权益是否生效，App 使用 RevenueCat（RevenueCat, Inc.）；它会依据 RevenueCat 隐私政策接收一个匿名 App 用户 ID、已购商品标识与权益状态。这些数据仅用于校验 Pro 权益，不与你的内容关联，也不用于跨 App 或跨网站追踪你。我们不运营服务器，App 不会把收据或你写的内容发送给我们。

Purchases are linked to your Apple ID, not to a device. You can restore purchases inside the Pro screen on any device signed in with the same Apple ID, and manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

购买绑定在你的 Apple ID 上，而不是设备上。你可以在任何使用同一 Apple ID 登录的设备上，于 Pro 页面点击「恢复购买」；也可以在 iOS 设置 → Apple ID → 订阅 中管理或取消订阅。仅删除 App 不会取消订阅。

5. Your choices

5. 你的选择

  • Purchases: manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; restore purchases inside the Pro screen.
  • Export: Settings → Backup & Restore → Export backup. The file is plain text and readable by anyone who obtains it.
  • Delete an entry: remove it inside the app at any time.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete all data. This erases local entries and app-generated backups. It cannot be undone.
  • Uninstall: removing the app removes its local storage and encryption key.
  • 购买：在 iOS 设置 → Apple ID → 订阅 中管理或取消订阅；在 Pro 页面点击「恢复购买」。
  • 导出：设置 → 备份与恢复 → 导出备份。文件是明文，任何取得它的人都能读取。
  • 删除单条：随时在 App 内删除。
  • 删除全部：设置 → 数据与隐私 → 清空全部数据。这会清除本地内容与 App 生成的备份，且不可撤销。
  • 卸载：删除 App 会移除本地存储与加密密钥。

6. Data requests

6. 数据请求

We hold no journal content and cannot provide, correct, or delete a copy of it — there is nothing on our side to act on. Your device is the only place the content exists. For purchase records, Apple and RevenueCat hold the data described in section 4; you can manage subscriptions through your Apple ID, and contact us if you need help locating the right request route.

我们不持有任何内容，因此无法提供、更正或删除内容副本——我们这边没有可操作的数据。内容只存在于你的设备上。购买记录方面，Apple 与 RevenueCat 持有第 4 节所述的数据；你可以通过 Apple ID 管理订阅，如需帮助确认该向哪一方申请，可联系我们。

7. Children

7. 儿童

The app is not directed to children under 13. Because no personal information is collected, no parental consent process is required. If a child has entered sensitive information on a shared device, delete the entry or uninstall the app.

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。由于不收集任何个人信息，无需家长同意流程。如果儿童在共用设备上写入了敏感信息，删除对应内容或卸载 App 即可。

8. Changes to this policy

8. 政策变更

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, where the change is material, ask you to review it inside the app.

若本政策发生变更，我们会更新上方生效日期；若变更重大，会在 App 内请你重新查看。
